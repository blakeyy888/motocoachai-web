Why this works

Not a chatbot. A coach.

Generic AI sports tools tell you your form looks "good" or "needs work." Moto Coach AI tells you exactly what a pro would catch — phase by phase, in motocross terms, with a drill to fix it.

01

Real Cueing Language

Stacked elbows, inside leg high, attack position, weight forward through the chop. The model speaks the language of motocross — because that's how it was taught.

02

Phase-by-Phase Reads

Corners break into entry, apex, and exit. Jumps break into approach, takeoff, and landing. You don't get one vague rating — you get a read on every phase.

03

Measured, Not Guessed

Lean angle, body extension, leg position, recovery time — extracted from real frames. Numbers behind the call. So you can argue with it — and learn from it.

04

A Drill, Every Time

Every session ends with a recommended drill targeted at the weakest phase. Show up to the track with something specific to fix — not "ride better."
